# Driver Load Log privacy policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Driver Load Log (app.driverloadlog) keeps your loads, miles, stops, pay settings, and tax settings on your phone. The developer does not collect this data and does not share it.

## What stays on the phone

- Loads, miles, stops, tarp pay, and dates you enter
- Pay, tax, insurance, and weekly bills settings
- Display choices such as text color

There is no account. There is no location. The app does not use ads or analytics, and it has no internet access.

## Importing and saving files

If you import data from your old log, you pick the file and it is read on your phone. If you save or share a file, you choose where it goes. The app does not send anything anywhere on its own.

## Delete

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, erases Driver Load Log data on this phone.
